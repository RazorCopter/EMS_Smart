## Strategy boundaries

| Strategy | Intended role |
|---|---|
| Self Use | React to measured PCC exchange. Under the canonical signs use `P_batt_measured - (P_grid_measured - P_grid_target)` as the first-order candidate. Validate AC/DC response and loss assumptions. |
| Charge First | Prioritize charging immediately from available PV surplus until the configured limit/target, with separately permitted grid assist. |
| Grid Optimized Charge | Delay/limit charging intentionally to reserve headroom, guided by forecast and export constraint. |
| Target SOC | Plan a requested SOC by a deadline, reporting energy deficit and reachability. |
| Maintenance | Explicit bounded maintenance schedule; eligible charge/hold/completion/abort states and return to normal. Any high target and hold must match manufacturer/BMS evidence. |
| Time Of Use | Schedule from tariffs and availability. v5.1 baseline models balancing, hold discharge, optional policy-controlled grid charge and discharge to local consumption. Delay/limit charge and avoiding export clipping may use Grid Optimized Charge inputs. Peak Shaving and Discharge Grid are outside baseline scope. |
| Native Inverter | Observe the inverter's own operating behavior and emit no candidate. |
| Safe Mode | Safety-selected veto/deadline fallback for unusable essentials, no executable plan, audit cause. |

A strategy produces a typed intent or candidate only. SOC reserve belongs to Safety across strategies. G6 defines the local deterministic slice first, then qualifies target and maintenance. Forecast and tariff paths require replay coverage and accept real advice only after G7.
